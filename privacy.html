Usage! — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 14, 2026

The short version

Usage! collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, and no network transmission of your data.

What the app reads

To show you device statistics, the app reads — on your device only — storage capacity, memory statistics, CPU load, battery level and charging state, network interface byte counters and IP addresses, device model, iOS version, and system boot time. These values are displayed to you and stored locally on your device (and in your private iCloud key-value storage if you enable Cloud Sync) so history charts work. They never reach us or anyone else.

Estimates

Battery capacity in mAh is an estimate derived from your device model's published design capacity; it is not a measurement of your battery's health.

Purchases

Usage! Pro is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles transactions.
